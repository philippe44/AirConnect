Thank you for your interest in contributing. I have one rule: I will not accept “vibecoded” PRs.

The number of submissions is growing, and I cannot review and fix them all. Please only submit a PR if you have development experience and understand C/C++.

There is nothing wrong with using AI for development—I use it frequently myself. But you must be able to understand, analyze, and correct its output in the language you are working with. You are responsible for the code you submit, regardless of how it was written.

Observing the behavior of generated code and repeatedly prompting an AI to tweak it through trial and error is not enough. You need to understand why your changes work, explain your decisions, and address review feedback yourself. Otherwise, the burden of understanding and maintaining your contribution falls entirely on me, and I cannot take that on.
